# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring leads, design leaders, and product teams evaluating Seva Kudryavtsev's
work. They need to understand the quality of his consumer interfaces and the
systems thinking behind complex products quickly and with confidence.

## Product Purpose

Present selected product-design work through concise, image-led cases. Success
means that visitors can connect craft, product reasoning, and authorship without
the portfolio interface competing with the work itself.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, expressive. The experience should feel authored and deliberate,
with motion used to clarify relationships and hierarchy.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio templates and interchangeable editorial styling.
- Decorative complexity that competes with project imagery.
- Long or theatrical transitions that make navigation feel slower.
- Motion added for spectacle without explaining structure or continuity.

## Design Principles

- Let project work remain the strongest visual signal.
- Explain relationships through spatial continuity and restrained motion.
- Preserve direct, predictable navigation even when transitions are ambitious.
- Make consumer-interface craft visible while keeping systems thinking legible.
- Prefer one clear source of interaction state over distributed visual effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic content, and a
complete reduced-motion alternative. Motion must never be required to understand
navigation or content.
